Crystal Legacy gives every player access to four spells that define the pace and style of combat throughout the event. Your active spells are always visible in a HUD panel at the bottom-right of your screen, listed from bottom to top: **Dash**, **Bola de Fuego**, **Levioso**, and **Hachazo**. Each spell has its own cooldown timer that appears on the HUD so you always know when you can cast again. By default the four spells are bound to **R**, **F**, **G**, and **C** respectively — but every binding is fully rebindable under **Options → Controls → Key Binds → "Crystal Legacy | Teclas"**.

<Tip>
  Spells are unlocked during the **Tutorial** phase through unlock minigames. You start the event without any spells — completing the Tutorial minigames is how you earn them.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Spells deal no damage anywhere in the event unless PVP combat has been specifically enabled for two players by the tournament system or an operator. Outside of an active PVP match, all spell effects are non-lethal.
</Note>

## Spell Details

### Dash

Dash is a short-range burst of speed that propels you forward a short distance. It carries a short cooldown, making it one of your most frequently available tools. Dash is the **only spell that does not require the wand** — you can use it at any time regardless of what you are holding. Use it to close gaps, escape pressure, or reposition quickly in both exploration and combat scenarios.

### Bola de Fuego

Bola de Fuego launches a fireball projectile from the tip of your wand. The fireball travels in a straight path and **explodes on impact with anything it touches** — terrain, structures, or other players. You must be holding the wand to cast this spell. It is a strong offensive option, but its cooldown means you need to make each shot count.

### Levioso

Levioso causes the targeted player to **levitate** when you aim and cast it correctly. You must be holding the wand and have the target in your crosshair when you activate the spell. A well-timed Levioso can lift an opponent out of position, interrupt their movement, or set up a follow-up attack. Precision matters — if your aim is off, the effect will not apply.

### Hachazo

Hachazo is a devastating axe-slam attack that deals **high damage** and has a correspondingly **long cooldown**. You must be holding the wand to cast it. When Hachazo connects with a player target, the spell **consumes itself** — the charge is spent and the cooldown begins. Because the spell is consumed on hit, timing and accuracy are critical: commit to the strike only when you are confident it will land.
