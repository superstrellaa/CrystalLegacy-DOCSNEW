The `WAITING_PLAYERS` state is the first phase players experience after the server opens its doors. Every player who connects sees a dedicated waiting screen that signals the event has not started yet. This phase is intentionally quiet — voice chat is automatically muted server-wide so players do not talk over each other on the lobby screen. Your job during this phase is simply to confirm that all 30 players have joined before advancing.

## Entering WAITING_PLAYERS

Transition the server into the waiting lobby with:

```bash
/master estado WAITING_PLAYERS
```

## Player Experience

When a player connects during this state they are immediately shown the waiting-for-players screen. They cannot interact with the event, cast spells, or hear other players through voice chat. The screen persists until you advance to the next phase.

## Voice Chat Behavior

Crystal Legacy automatically manages the `voicechat.speak` permission during this phase:

- **On entering WAITING_PLAYERS** — voice chat is disabled for all connected players. No player can speak or hear others through Simple Voice Chat.
- **On leaving WAITING_PLAYERS** — voice chat is automatically re-enabled for all players as the server transitions into `TUTORIAL`. You do not need to restore permissions manually.

## Managing the Waiting Screen Per Player

You can show or hide the waiting screen for individual players or groups without changing the global state:

```bash
/master pantalla esperando mostrar ;player(s);
```

```bash
/master pantalla esperando ocultar ;player(s);
```

Replace `;player(s);` with a player name or a comma-separated list of names.

## Advancing to TUTORIAL

Once all 30 players are connected and you are ready to begin, advance to the next phase:

```bash
/master estado TUTORIAL
```

<Tip>
  Monitor your current connected player count at any time with `/master info`. This command returns a live snapshot of connected players, their registration status, and other server-side event data — handy for confirming your roster is complete before you move on.
</Tip>
