Crystal Legacy takes place on a custom map designed for up to 30 players, and getting around it quickly is part of playing well. You have two primary ways to move beyond standard walking: the **Dash spell** for fast short-range bursts and the **warp command** for instant teleportation to named map locations. Knowing when to use each option will help you navigate the map efficiently in every phase of the event.

<Tip>
  Consider rebinding Dash to a key you find comfortable under **Options → Controls → Key Binds → "Crystal Legacy | Teclas"**. Since Dash is your most frequently used movement tool, having it on an easy-to-reach key makes a real difference.
</Tip>

## Dash Spell

Dash is your go-to movement ability. Pressing the default keybind **R** launches you forward in a short-range burst, letting you cover ground quickly, dodge incoming spells, or close the gap on an opponent. Dash has a short cooldown, so it comes back frequently. It is the only spell in Crystal Legacy that **does not require the wand** — you can Dash with anything in your hand, or even empty-handed.

See the [Spells](/gameplay/spells) page for full details on Dash and all other spells.

## Warp Command

To teleport to a named location on the map, use the warp command:

```bash
/master warp ;location;
```

Replace `;location;` with the name of the warp point you want to reach. This command is available to all players and can be used at any time outside of restricted event phases. Warp points are set up by operators and cover key areas of the Crystal Legacy map such as spawn zones, arenas, and event areas.
